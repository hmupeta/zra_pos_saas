Running multiple branches shouldn't feel like guesswork

Without a unified system, growth creates blind spots. Here is what we solve.

No real-time visibility

You don't know today's sales at each branch until end-of-day reports arrive — often manually compiled.

Inconsistent pricing

Different branches charge different prices for the same item because product lists are managed separately.

No inter-branch stock transfers

Branch A runs out of stock while Branch B has excess — but there's no system to facilitate transfers.

Separate customer databases

A loyal customer at Branch A is unknown to Branch B — a lost loyalty opportunity.

Manual consolidated reporting

Someone must pull reports from each branch and merge them — weekly or monthly.

Inconsistent promotions

Running the same promotion across all branches simultaneously is difficult to coordinate.

Everything you need to run a modern retail chain

One platform. Every tool. Zero friction.

Centralized Multi-Branch Control

Manage every location from one HQ dashboard. Compare performance, push pricing updates instantly, and monitor sales in real-time.

Inter-Branch Stock Transfers

Move stock between locations with a full audit trail. Inventory counts update automatically on both sides when a transfer is confirmed.

Multi-Tenant Data Isolation

Each business operates in its own secure silo. Your data, customers, and finances are never shared with other tenants.

Unified Financial Reporting

Consolidated profit & loss, sales summaries, and inventory valuations across all branches with a single click. No manual merging.

Centralized Customer Database

A customer registered at any branch is recognized at every branch. Loyalty points, purchase history, and credit limits follow them everywhere.

Branch-Level Access Control

Cashiers see only their till. Managers see their branch. Owners see everything. Granular role-based permissions keep your data secure.

From chaos to clarity in four steps

Setting up your retail empire shouldn't require a consultant.

01

Set Up Locations

Add your branches with addresses, contacts, and operating hours. Each gets its own settings while sharing a master catalog.

02

Assign Users

Add staff to their respective branches. Managers get multi-branch access; cashiers stay locked to their till.

03

Sync Inventory

Upload products once and distribute to locations. Set initial stock levels per branch and let transfers handle the rest.

04

Monitor & Grow

Watch sales roll in from HQ. Respond to opportunities and issues instantly with real-time data.
