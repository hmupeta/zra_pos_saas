1. Introduction

Nimbus POS ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information in compliance with the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 of the Republic of Zambia [citation:2] and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act No. 4 of 2021 [citation:3].

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal data:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and business details when you register.
  • Transaction Data: Sales records, inventory details, and payment information processed through the POS.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, and interaction with our platform.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under the Data Protection Act, we process your personal data based on:

  • Consent: Where you have given explicit consent for processing [citation:6].
  • Contract: Where processing is necessary to fulfill our service agreement with you.
  • Legal Obligation: Where we must comply with Zambian laws, including ZRA fiscalization requirements.

4. Data Storage and Cross-Border Transfers

In compliance with the Data Protection Act, personal data is primarily processed and stored on servers located within the Republic of Zambia [citation:9]. If we use cloud services hosted outside Zambia, we ensure that such transfers are subject to standard contractual clauses approved by the Data Protection Commissioner, or we obtain your explicit consent for the transfer [citation:9][citation:14].

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Object to or restrict processing.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

6. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. This includes encryption and secure access controls [citation:13].
